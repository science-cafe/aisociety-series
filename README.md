# aisociety-series
A 90-minute experience room where youth ambassadors, scientists, researchers and museum educators sit at the same table to ask how artificial intelligence is reshaping the way we learn, work and decide.

# Science Café 
Science Café is a space where curious people come together to talk about science. No lectures, no slides — just a great topic, good questions, and a room full of people who want to understand the world better. Science Café is a part of the [SciComm Initiative](https://scicomm-initiative.github.io/scicomm-site/index.html), making science vivid, human, and worth talking about.

**For more information about Science Cafe** [click on the URL](https://scicomm-initiative.github.io/scicomm-site/cafe.html).
