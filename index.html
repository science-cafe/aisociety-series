01 · About the café

No lectures. No slides. Just good questions about AI.

A Science Café is a space where curious people come together to talk about science. This inaugural edition focuses on one of the most consequential questions of our time: how artificial intelligence and society shape each other.

Rather than a panel of experts speaking to an audience, everyone participates. Short introductory remarks set the scene, then participants move into small groups to explore prompted questions — bringing together the perspectives of young people, working scientists, university researchers and museum educators.

With participants' consent, what's said in the room becomes something lasting: educational materials for the Smithsonian Science Education Center and a public recap for the SciComm Initiative.

04 · The experience room

How the 90 minutes work

Step 1

Set the scene

Brief introductory remarks frame why AI and society matters now, and what we hope to learn together.

Step 2

Break into small groups

Participants split into mixed groups of four to five, each with a facilitator and a set of prompted questions.

Step 3

Share across tables

Each group reports its sharpest insight and its biggest open question back to the full room.

Step 4

Capture, with consent

Participants choose whether and how their contributions are shared in the educational materials and blog post.
